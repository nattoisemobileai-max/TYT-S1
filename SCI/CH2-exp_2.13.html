📚 Key Concepts 課本重點 (p.125)
• Chlorination (加氯消毒法): Adding chlorine to water to kill most microorganisms; widely used in water treatment plants (濾水廠) and swimming pools (游泳池).
• Caution: Chlorine is toxic and has an irritating smell (有毒及具刺激性氣味). Excessive chlorine irritates skin and eyes (刺激皮膚和眼睛).
