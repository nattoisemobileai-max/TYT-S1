🎯 Aim / Purpose 實驗目的
To build a laboratory model simulating parts of the water cycle (evaporation, condensation, and rainfall), observe the colour of the collected 'rain', and verify whether the total mass of water in the system stays constant.
在實驗室建立模擬水循環（蒸發、凝結及降雨）的模型，觀察收集所得「雨水」的顏色，並驗證系統內水的總質量是否保持不變。

🌿 Key Point: Water Cycle 水循環重點 (p.88)
1️⃣ Seas/rivers absorb energy from the Sun 吸收太陽熱能
2️⃣ Water evaporates to become water vapour 蒸發成水蒸氣
3️⃣ Water vapour cools & condenses into clouds 遇冷凝結成雲
4️⃣ Water droplets fall as rain, snow or hail 降下成雨、雪或雹

⚠️ Safety Caution 安全注意事項 (p.87)
⚠️ Caution 注意事項
  • Handle the hot pebbles and hot water with care. Use tongs when transferring warmed pebbles into the transparent plastic container to avoid burns.
    小心處理熱卵石和熱水。把加熱後的卵石放入透明塑膠容器時，應使用鉗子夾取，以免燙傷。
  • Ensure the zipper bag of ice cubes is sealed tightly on the metal dish so melted ice water cannot drip into the container.
    確保裝有冰粒的密實袋緊密封好並放在金屬碟上，防止冰水滲入容器內影響實驗結果。
