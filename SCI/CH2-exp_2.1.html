🎯 Aim / Purpose 實驗目的
To measure the temperature changes of crushed ice as it is heated until it melts into liquid water and boils into steam, and to plot a heating curve (Temperature vs. Time).

⚠️ Safety & Experimental Tips 安全與貼士 (p.80–81)
🥽 Wear safety goggles 佩戴安全護目鏡 & Caution 注意事項
  • Wear safety goggles throughout the heating experiment.
    整個加熱實驗期間必須佩戴安全護目鏡。
  • Do not clamp the thermometer too tightly. Otherwise, you will break it.
    切勿把溫度計夾得太緊，否則會弄破溫度計。
  • Allow the beaker to cool before removing it from the tripod after the experiment.
    實驗後，須待燒杯冷卻才可把它從三腳架上移開。
💡 Experimental Tips 實驗小貼士
  • Page 80 Tip: Make sure the bulb of the thermometer is completely immersed in the crushed ice and does not touch the bottom of the beaker.
    確保溫度計的液泡完全浸沒在碎冰中，且沒有觸及燒杯底部。
  • Page 81 Tip: Each time you record the temperature of the water, stir it with a glass rod to make sure that the temperature is the same throughout the water.
    每次記錄水溫前，先用玻璃棒攪拌，確保整杯水的溫度均勻一致。
