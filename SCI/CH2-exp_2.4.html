Exp 2.4 Soluble & Insoluble Substances 分類可溶及不可溶物質
💧 Washing spoon & glass rod... 正在清洗藥匙及玻璃棒...
🎯 Aim, Hypothesis & Apparatus 目的、假設與器材
🎯 Aim / Purpose 實驗目的
To investigate whether different substances (table salt, corn flour, rice, jelly powder, and soil) dissolve in water, and to classify them as soluble or insoluble substances.
探究不同物質（食鹽、粟粉、米粒、啫喱粉及泥土）能否溶於水中，並把它們分類為可溶於水的物質或不可溶於水的物質。
